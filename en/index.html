01 · Graduation plan

Start with the answer.

Add your record PDF from the university system, or type your courses in. Fasli reads the file, you check every line, and the Plan tab opens with the term you can graduate. Then come the details: what to take next, which courses unlock others, and what changes if you drop a course or skip a summer.

02 · The app

Five tabs. Each does one job.

Extra tools sit inside them and appear when you need them, so the app stays calm.

03 · Also inside

The small things, handled.

Day and week

  • Mark “No class” for one date, or add a make-up class
  • Busy hours for work or family, kept out of suggested study time
  • Lectures, exams and deadlines in your phone’s calendar
  • Gregorian and Hijri dates

Exams

  • Exam day: a countdown, a checklist and the late-entry rule
  • A finals revision planner that spreads your study hours
  • Official academic calendar and exam dates
  • A lock-screen countdown on class and exam days

Marks and standing

  • A warning before the last day to drop, if a course may not pass
  • GPA calculator with a target, and a clear way back to 2.00
  • Close the term after finals in one tap
  • A one-page progress report PDF for family or your sponsor

Reminders and tools

  • Reminders before lectures, deadlines and exams, with Done and “In 2 hours”
  • Focus timer and weekly study stats
  • Home-screen widgets
  • Five colour themes, on phones and tablets

04 · Your data

Your data stays yours.

No account needed

Fasli works without signing in. Sign in with Google only if you want backup and sync.

Read on your phone first

Record PDFs are usually read on your phone, and only the courses and grades are sent for planning. If the phone can’t read a file, it goes to our server and is deleted right after.

No university password

Fasli never asks for your university password.

No ads

Delete your data or your account from Settings at any time.
